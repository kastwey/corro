# Starter Categories

Everyone writes at the same time. A round deals one letter and a few categories, and every answer must start with that letter. The round's judge does not write: once the writing stops, they rule on what everybody else wrote. The job rotates, so each player judges the same number of rounds.

The host chooses one shared content language from the package's real decks. Every player writes to the same categories, and draws from the same letters, even when their interface languages differ.

The judge starts the clock when the table has read the letter and the categories. Writers fill one box per category; answers save as they are typed, and finishing early moves the round on as soon as everybody has.

## Playing with a screen reader

Focus enters your own job: your sheet while the clock runs, the answers while they are being judged, and Escape always returns to it. While writing, Enter moves to the next category and, from the last one, focuses the finish button without pressing it; the surface's letter shortcuts never fire from inside a box you could be typing in. While judging, Up and Down move between the answers — each reads as one sentence naming who wrote it, what they wrote and what Corro noticed about it — and Right enters that answer's three verdicts, with Left or Escape to come back out. Press R to hear the remaining time, S for your score and role, Shift+S for the other players' scores, T for what round this is and what you should be doing, Ctrl+F6 to cycle panels and Ctrl+Shift+R for text chat.
